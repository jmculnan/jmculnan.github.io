# About

I am a natural language processing (NLP) researcher with experience building and maintaining NLP pipelines at scale. 
I work as the NLP team lead within the Boston Informatics Group - Fillmore Informatics Team at the Massachusetts Veterans Epidemiology Research and Information Collaborative (MAVERIC) at VA Boston.

## Recent projects 

My recent work focuses on the application of NLP to electronic health records, primarily with the end goal of extracting cancer-related data elements with large language models (LLMs) and rule-based models.
